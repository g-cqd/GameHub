Move the back-buffer lock's rect geometry into surface_lock with host tests

Ports the core half of 5c91c3d onto the merged back-buffer lock path:
`region_offset` (where a writable lock's rect starts inside the
whole-surface page, refused when it lies outside the page) and
`writeback_regions` (the source rect and its render-resolution
counterpart for the write-back, so only the locked rect is resampled
under render.scale) move into mtld3d-core with unit tests, and
surface.rs calls them instead of its inline copies. Behaviour is
unchanged except that an offset equal to the page length is now
refused rather than mapping an empty tail.
