# 2026-10-07 (01): The native arm64 Wine route

*Reconstructed on 2026-10-31 from the commit history of the five repositories; nothing here was re-run.*


An experimental arm64 Wine route:
- a 4 GiB soft page zero;
- the x18 TEB protocol, with PE code in custom-x18 mode;
- 4K-page spawn attributes for the loader only;
- a CAMetalLayer instead of the x86_64-only D3DMetal hook.

On the bundler side, an arm64 winehost helper app with its own entitlements and audit, a display-safety rule,
crash-report building and a bounded diagnostic log.
