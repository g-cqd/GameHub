# 2026-10-31 (02): The DXMT staging allocator

*Reconstructed on 2026-10-31 from the commit history of the five repositories; nothing here was re-run.*


DXMT's staging memory became a ring, an arena and a power-of-two pool, and the resource initializer submits uploads
by a 128 MB budget (ADR-0016). Three follow-up fixes:
- reference-tracker storage uses the offset the allocator returns;
- a retired ring is released after its newest chunk, not after its last allocation's;
- large blocks are pooled instead of one-off FIFO blocks.

The trigger was Crysis 2 Remastered allocating 181 × 64 MB (11 GB) during a level load. The logic is unit-tested
natively.
