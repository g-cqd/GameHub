# 2026-10-31 (05): Wolfenstein II starts and stops flashing; ADR-0014 revised

**ADR-0014:** the owner set the line on protected games: run games with their native environment at full parity
with Windows, even when that means misreporting the hardware to a game that never considered Macs. The purpose is
playing, never an advantage. ADR-0014 and white paper §5 were rewritten around this.

**Wolfenstein II (roadmap 0029):**
- It had never been launched (no Steam game action). The first launch hit the Steam launch-option chooser and the
  game's EULA, both answered by the owner.
- The game died in `vkCreateDevice` with `VK_ERROR_FEATURE_NOT_PRESENT`. MoltenVK's log named the 39th feature flag.
  MoltenVK's source shows that ordinal counts from 1, which makes it `shaderCullDistance`.
- Wine 53e3e70 masks unsupported core features. The game then reached its menus.
- The owner reported heavy flashing. Six screenshots 120 ms apart showed one wholly black frame; they were deleted
  after viewing.
- A `+macdrv` trace showed a `client_surface_update` and a present on every frame, a surface detach and destroy, and
  `Present returned status 1000001003` (SUBOPTIMAL) from MoltenVK. MoltenVK's `hasOptimalSurface()` requires the image
  size to equal the layer's natural size unless the swapchain is deliberately scaled. Wine only requested scaling
  when the image differed from the client rect.
- Wine 48554b1 always requests it. Afterwards: 2 SUBOPTIMAL at startup, none after, and no surface teardown.

**Hub:** `--debug` was ignored when the store client was already running, because only `--metalfx` and `--dxgi`
counted as a changed launcher environment. `debug` now counts too.

**Installed, and left running:** win32u.so went into hub-2 by rm then cp. The owner is playing; nothing was restarted
after that.

**Open:** the mouse (described above) and the 5120x2880 desktop.
