The busiest day of the bundler line (73 commits).

- **Far Cry 2:** a renderer cache key for its shader cache, kept in the player folder.

  replaced by `~` in the shipped docs.

- **x87sidecar:** `fld_gap_fstp` fuses FLD m, independent non-x87 instructions and FSTP m into one copy. The research
  probes for stock-Rosetta gaps were also written.
