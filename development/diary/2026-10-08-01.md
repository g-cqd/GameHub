# 2026-10-08 (01): W^X emulation for Rosetta's late resume, and the dsound resampler

*Reconstructed on 2026-10-31 from the commit history of the five repositories; nothing here was re-run.*


- **The cause:** Rosetta 2 resumes late after two stores rewrite the next instruction. x87sidecar's research has the
  minimal reproducer.
- **The Wine workaround:** optional W^X emulation for private RWX pages. It keeps the trap flag out of guest-visible
  contexts, handles stores that straddle pages, and can log per-page history.
- **Delivery:** shipped in a series of NFS 2015 runtimes, `-0005` to `-0010`. It is on by default from `-0009`, and
  each delivery was recorded with what was and wasn't verified.
- **Also in Wine:**
  - the SSE2 dsound resampler with a per-phase coefficient cache;
  - Darwin-ABI stack-slot adapters for syscalls with more than 8 arguments;
  - richer crash blocks in kernelbase.
- **Licensing:** a third-party inventory, a NOTICE and an LGPL source offer were drafted.
