# 2026-10-31 (01): Steam's 64-bit client, Watch_Dogs, Ubisoft in software

*Reconstructed on 2026-10-31 from the commit history of the five repositories; nothing here was re-run.*


- **Steam:** the current 64-bit client renders, signs in and updates itself on hub-2, so the update block and the
  anti-update flags are gone (ADR-0010). Steam games run on hub-2 by default.
- **Watch_Dogs:** the Ubisoft overlay is kept out of the game. Its hook spun on the overlay mutex before the D3D
  device existed, and it only went on once another game had started.
- **Ubisoft Connect:** renders in software, because its in-process GPU drawing glitched on hub-2's DXMT (ADR-0012).
- **Wine:** dinput foreground acquire fixes; winecoreaudio shared-mode streams use the mix format's channel count and
  report starvation.
