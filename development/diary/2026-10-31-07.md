# 2026-10-31 (07): HITMAN 3 runs, after a loop that should have been a reference comparison

HITMAN 3 (D3D12 only) ran through three D3D12 paths:
- **Wine's vkd3d:** aborts at the pipeline-library check.
- **DXMT's d3d12** (enabled and fixed along the way: pipeline libraries, feature answers, shader-model lowering, PIX
  markers, mid-block discard): stops at DXIL wave operations.
- **D3DMetal:** this one runs.

**The loop.** D3DMetal came first from Apple's GPTK 4.0b3 download, then 3.0. Each run hung, and each symptom got its
own experiment:
- the NVIDIA shim removed;
- Streamline's Reflex disabled, by environment and then by registry override;
- a debugger attach;
- relay traces.

The owner stopped it ("you're looping on things, take a step back").

**What the step back found.** The drive already had the reference: a CrossOver 26.3 Wine build with D3DMetal
integrated (`toolchains/wine-sdk-cx-26.3.0-5`). Its D3DMetal is 4.0b2, not the 3.0 our notes claimed. Its glue is
different: CrossOver's own PE DLLs, plus `.so` symlinks to one `libd3dshared.dylib` in `lib/external`. Apple's redist
glue targets Apple's evaluation Wine. With CrossOver's layout copied exactly, HITMAN 3 started, opened its fullscreen
window and plays.

**Two side findings:**
- `+module` tracing makes Steam die at start (an exception frame outside the stack).
- `hub play` stops after restarting Steam whenever Steam needs more than 120 s to log on; the retry then restarts it
  again. Fix the launcher.

**Lesson, now in the way of working:** when something has a known-good integration (CrossOver, a previous runtime, a
working bundle), reproduce that first and diff against it. Don't assemble from parts and debug the symptoms.
