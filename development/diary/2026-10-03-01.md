# 2026-10-03 (01): Settings pages, the EA client layer, Authenticode

*Reconstructed on 2026-10-31 from the commit history of the five repositories; nothing here was re-run.*


- **NFS 2015 settings page:** it changes only validated lines of the game's own options file. A MetalFX group is
  default-off and gated to the options DXMT v0.80 documents.
- **The EA client layer:** a policy-checked, reproducible capture of EA's installer, vetted by an Authenticode check
  that refuses:
  - trailing bytes;
  - SHA-1;
  - a chain that doesn't end in the pinned root.

  The layer is applied on first start as verified clones plus registry entries appended to the stopped prefix's hives.
- **Licences:** DXMT's MIT licence ships in every app.
