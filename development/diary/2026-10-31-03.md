# 2026-10-31 (03): Static analysis of every project, and every real finding fixed

The owner asked for a review of every research document, commit and agent session in scope. That was 19 sessions:
3 Claude, 6 Codex, 9 pi and 1 ask; the Gemini sessions were generic and out of scope. Then came a static-only bug
hunt with real checkers. Midway the owner said "no we want to fix everything", so upstream lines in the files we
touch were fixed too when the finding was real.

**Checkers** (ADR-0015, now in `scripts/static-analysis/`):
- The clang analyzer over the real compile commands of the 41 Wine files our commits touched. The first pass reached
  20 objects, the second the other 27.
- clang-tidy through the DXMT and x87sidecar compile databases.
- cppcheck.
- clippy for mtld3d.
- ruff, pyright and shellcheck for the hub.

**Fixed:**
- **Wine** (941e02e…ff91cd1):
  - environment `realloc` checks;
  - the x87 sidecar exec: a checked argv allocation, the strdup leak, the ERR→TRACE log level, and a WARN on a failed
    `execv`;
  - zero-initialised image info for ntdll and wow64 ntdll;
  - a NULL home directory;
  - the PATH copy and `loader-wow64` path leaks;
  - `memcpy` from NULL SD parts;
  - `malloc(0)` in NtFilterToken and dinput;
  - dsound's zero denominator;
  - an ncrypt property that lost its key.
- **DXMT** (d907082): an ERR for a dropped initialization, `out_of_range` caught, and "forced to allocate new block"
  moved to INFO.
- **x87sidecar** (4f2e62e): a dead and wrong `emit_add_reg` encoding, the uint16_t opcode loop, and an unchecked FCmp
  input.
- **mtld3d** (4a2f494): clippy.
- **Hub** (b2455c8, d275a82, 4677479):
  - a crash when the EA app is missing;
  - the native panel size taken from the main display's native-flagged mode instead of the first ioreg match;
  - leaked log handles;
  - `die()` declared NoReturn, which removed about 40 pyright false positives;
  - the shellcheck and ruff findings.

**False positives, recorded:**
- **Wine:** dwarf.h; server.c (`dup`, the `memcpy` of the context); system.c; signal_x86_64.c (the Darwin float state
  is 524 bytes); `mach.c`'s `info`; locale_private.h.
- **DXMT:** airconv switch defaults; `TextureUpdateCommand`, whose callers check `Invalid`.
- **Hub:** the bundler's B023 hits (each closure runs inside its own iteration).
- **x87sidecar:** the IR invariants; the `mmap` VM tag; the `atomic_ref` parse errors, which are a Homebrew clang-tidy
  and Apple libc++ mismatch.

**Built, installed and not run:** ntdll.so, dsound, dinput, dinput8 and ncrypt (both architectures), the DXMT PEs and
x87sidecar went into hub-2 by rm then cp. Steam was running from hub-2 and keeps the old files until it restarts.
The 10 launcher tests pass. No game was started.
