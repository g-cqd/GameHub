  runtime in `share/wine/hub-rules.txt`, read before `compatdb.so` and synced into the PEB (ADR-0006).
- **Stores:**

  - **Ubisoft Connect:** launched through `upc.exe`. Its packer's selector-0x33 far jumps are rewritten when a rule
    asks (Wine 73516bb).
- **Wine:**

  - ncrypt persists ECDSA and ECDH keys;
  - `WINE_COMPATDB` is synced into the PEB.
- **x87sidecar:** ARPL is translated even in blocks that go to stock.
- **GTA V:**
  - `d3d11.preciseDivide`, NaN-safe options and FMA off removed the pipeline failures;
  - `strictMath` didn't help;
  - MSAA off removes the lattices;
  - late textures come from memory pressure.
- **Tools:** `watch_game.py` (disk, paging and screenshots with magenta detection) and `check_links.py`.
- **The repository** was sorted into `apps/bundler`, `docs/{hub,knowledge,history}` and `archive/`.
