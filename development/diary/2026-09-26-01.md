# 2026-09-26 (01): NFS 2015, Rosetta tracing, and the sidecar's allocator

*Reconstructed on 2026-10-31 from the commit history of the five repositories; nothing here was re-run.*


- **NFS 2015:** the work began as a bundled app. That covered bounded startup traces, runtime probes, Rosetta setup,
  and Developer ID signing and notarisation of "version 5" deliveries.
- **The EA app:** documented in an isolated prefix. A process-query error in NFS was ruled out with scalar tracing.
- **mtld3d:** immediate presents paced independently of the display refresh; vertex spacing and complete attribute
  reads preserved.
- **x87sidecar:**
  - translation buffers reserved through verified native allocators, with request buffers validated first;
  - the assembler's storage growth bounded;
  - cached registers invalidated whenever translation falls back to stock;
  - exact native x87 state boundaries made faster.
- **Branches:** every fork moved to one development branch that day, a first step toward ADR-0003.
