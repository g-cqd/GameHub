# 0023: MX Master 4 buttons and the horizontal wheel

- Buttons 5 and up are reported as F13–F20 (2f9ba59). Check them in the games that bind them.
- `SM_MOUSEHORIZONTALWHEELPRESENT` is not set.
- Native buttons need Options+ stopped and a power cycle (`docs/hub/mx-master-4.md`).

## Done when
A game sees the thumb wheel as a horizontal wheel and binds the extra buttons.
