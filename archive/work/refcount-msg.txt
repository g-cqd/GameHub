Retain an additional swap chain privately from its back buffer's public references

Wine's d3d9 conformance test_refcount (device.c:1781) reads an additional
swap chain's refcount as 1 after GetBackBuffer: the back buffer keeps its
chain alive without adding to the count the application sees. The fork's
model, kept through the upstream merge, took a public reference on the
chain at the back buffer's 0-to-1 edge, which this site caught once the
conformance suite ran against the merge. The back buffer's public edge now
retains the chain through its private count, as its binding references
already did, so the chain is still freed only once neither the
application nor the device references either object.
