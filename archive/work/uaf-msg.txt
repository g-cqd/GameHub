Hold the device across an additional back buffer's last Release

Now that the back buffer retains its swap chain privately, a chain the
application has released holds no device reference, so the back buffer's
forwarded reference can be the device's last one. Its final Release
would then tear the device down inside com_release and finalize the
chain after it, and the chain's finalize still reaches the device. A
temporary device reference brackets the chain decrement, so the device
is released after the chain.
